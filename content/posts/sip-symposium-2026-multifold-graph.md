I will present our work **深層展開を用いた Multifold グラフの学習と信号復元**
as a poster at the 41st Signal Processing Symposium (第41回信号処理シンポジウム),
held December 2–4, 2026 at 広島市文化交流会館, Hiroshima.

Poster session 1-P-1 (信号処理基礎・最適化・学習): Wednesday, December 2,
11:00–12:00, room ルミエール, poster 1-P-1-12.

This poster covers the same work as our ICASSP 2027 submission,
"Multivariate signal restoration via Multifold Graph Learning".

Authors: 横田 陽樹, 田中 雄一
